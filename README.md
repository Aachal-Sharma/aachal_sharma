### About me

I am an M.Tech by Research student at the **Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (CAIR), IIT Mandi**.

I work on mobile manipulation: a robot that navigates to an object, understands it, and grasps it, with LLM agents planning around obstacles and world models predicting how the scene will change. My experiments run on a Kinova Gen3 arm and an Intel RealSense D415.

- From **December 2026**, I will be a visiting researcher at **Keio University, Japan**, under the JST LOTUS Programme, working on agentic world models.
- I am looking for a **PhD position starting 2027** in world models and VLMs for robot manipulation.
